Hurry up, free Microsoft certification vouchers are available for Certification Week

Microsoft Certification Week is giving away 100% exam vouchers through LevelUp to people at Microsoft partner organisations. Complete a prep course, score 80% or more on one practice test, and the voucher is yours. Stock is limited, so do not leave it.

By Mahesh26 October 20266 min read

This one is not a fix or a known issue. It is simply advice, and it is time sensitive.

For Microsoft Certification Week, Microsoft is sponsoring free exam vouchers that cover the full cost of a role based certification exam, handed out through the LevelUp for Microsoft learning platform. I went through it myself this week. I completed a prep course, scored over 80% on the practice exam, and my voucher is secured. If you have been putting off a certification because of the exam fee, this is the time to stop putting it off.

The catch you need to know first

There is no fixed end date. The offer runs "until stocks last". It opened on 9 October 2026 and each eligible organisation only gets a limited number of vouchers. Once your company's allocation is gone, it is gone. If you are reading this, start today.

Who can get one

  • You must register with your work email address, and that email domain must belong to an organisation in the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program. Microsoft decides eligibility based on its partner list.
  • It is one voucher per person for this offer, no matter how many courses you complete.
  • Microsoft employees and Microsoft vendor accounts are not eligible for the free voucher.

If your employer is a Microsoft partner, you very likely qualify. If you are not sure, ask your partner or alliance team which email domain is registered with Microsoft.

Which exams are covered

The Certification Week offer covers these role based exams.

Area Exams
Endpoint and Modern Work MD-102 (Endpoint Administrator), MS-700, MS-721
Security SC-200, SC-401, SC-500
Azure and AI AZ-305, AI-200
Data and apps DP-600, PL-400, MB-800
Business and agents AB-100, AB-210, AB-250, AB-410
GitHub GH-600 (GitHub Copilot)

For readers of this blog, MD-102 is the obvious pick if you work with Intune and Windows, and SC-401 or SC-200 if you lean towards Purview or Defender. Fundamentals exams are not covered.

A couple of exams people often ask about, such as SC-300 and AZ-500, are not on this particular list, so check the catalogue before you enrol.

Step by step

Here is exactly what to do, with the links you need at each stage.

Part 1. Check you are eligible

  1. Confirm your company is a Microsoft partner. Your partner or alliance team will know. You can also search for your company in the Microsoft partner directory. If it is not a partner, the voucher will not be issued, so do not waste your practice attempt.
  2. Read the LevelUp voucher terms and conditions once. They list the eligible exams, the one attempt rule and the 60 day redemption window.

Part 2. Register on LevelUp

  1. Open the LevelUp Certification Prep course list. You will land on the LevelUp sign in page.
  2. Select Sign up.
  3. Register with your work email address, the one on your company's partner domain. Do not use Gmail, Outlook.com or any personal address, because eligibility is checked against the email domain.
  4. Confirm your email address from the activation mail LevelUp sends you, then sign in.
  5. Once signed in, open the Certification Prep course list again. You should now see the courses instead of the sign in page.

Part 3. Complete the course

  1. Pick one course for an exam on the eligible list. For Intune and Windows people that is the MD-102 course.
  2. Before anything else, complete the Welcome to cert prep module. It is required for the voucher, and it is the step people most often miss.
  3. Work through the course modules properly. Make notes on anything you do not use day to day.
  4. Use the free Microsoft Learn practice assessment for your exam as a warm up. You can take it as many times as you like. For example, the MD-102 exam page links to it. Aim for a comfortable pass there before you go near the LevelUp practice test.

Part 4. Take the practice test

  1. When you are ready, start the practice test in your LevelUp course. You get one attempt for this offer, and you need 80% or more.
  2. Do it in one sitting, on a reliable connection, with notifications off.

Part 5. Receive and redeem the voucher

  1. Wait for the voucher email from LevelUp. It arrives within 5 to 7 business days of passing. Check your spam and junk folders.
  2. Go to the Microsoft Learn page for your exam, for example MD-102, SC-401, SC-200 or AZ-305.
  3. Select Schedule exam and sign in with your Microsoft Learn profile. Use the same profile you want the certification to appear on, ideally a personal one so the certification stays with you if you change jobs.
  4. Continue to Pearson VUE, choose an online proctored exam or a test centre, and pick a date and time.
  5. At checkout, enter the voucher code from the LevelUp email. The price should drop to zero before you confirm.
  6. Do this within 60 days of receiving the voucher. After that the code expires.

Tips from going through it

  • Treat the practice test like the real thing. One attempt is all you get. Do the course, take notes and only start the test when you are ready. Do it in one sitting with no distractions.
  • Pick the exam that matches your day job. A practice test is much easier to pass on a subject you already work with every day.
  • Book the real exam soon after the voucher arrives. Sixty days goes faster than you think, and the knowledge is fresh straight after the course.
  • If the voucher code fails when you book, for example "Invalid voucher", contact the LevelUp helpdesk through the LevelUp terms page rather than Pearson VUE.

Why bother

Certifications are not everything, but MD-102 in particular is a good structured way to fill the gaps in Intune that day to day work never forces you to learn, such as Autopilot edge cases, app protection and compliance design. And free is free.

Once you have it, add it to LinkedIn and your CV. If this post helped you get a voucher, let me know in the comments which exam you went for.
